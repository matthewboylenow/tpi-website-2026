## General Style Rules

Apply these across all pages, including any not covered above (Blog, What's New, Work With Us, Meet Your Salesperson):

1. **No em dashes.** Use periods or commas. Em dashes are one of the most recognizable AI writing tells.

2. **Kill "we don't just X, we Y" and "not only X, but Y."** These are AI construction patterns. Just say what you do.

3. **Drop "your business" as filler.** Most of the time it can just be cut.

4. **No fake-casual warmth.** "No pressure," "we'd love to," "let's chat," and "we're here for you" sound like a chatbot. TPI's voice is friendly but matter-of-fact.

5. **Lead with what the equipment does, not how it makes the customer feel.** "Turn your bar into a destination" tells the reader nothing. "Frozen drinks that pour the same every time" tells them everything.
